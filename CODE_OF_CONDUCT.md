# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and technically constructive. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised conduct, and publishing another
person's confidential information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@ingestron.io`. The project owner may
edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation to protect people
and the project. This small technical preview does not promise a response SLA or
independent appeals process.
